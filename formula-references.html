📊 Risk Calculation Methodologies

📈 AIRB Approach

✅ Complete

Advanced Internal Ratings Based - Uses internal models for credit risk estimation

  • Internal PD/LGD estimation
  • Maturity adjustment factors
  • Correlation adjustment (PD-based)
  • Complex formula calculation
  • Requires regulatory approval
  • Minimum 5-7 years historical data
📚 View Details

📋 Standardized Approach

✅ Complete

Rating-Based Approach - Uses external ratings for risk weight determination

  • External rating required
  • Fixed risk weight lookup
  • Simple RWA formula
  • Collateral adjustment support
  • No regulatory approval needed
  • 21 rating levels (AAA-D)
📚 View Details

⚙️ Operational Risk

⏳ Planned - Phase 3

Risk from failed processes, people, systems, or events - Three approaches available

  • Basic Indicator Approach (BIA)
  • Standardized Approach (TSA)
  • Advanced Measurement (AMA)
  • Income-based calculations
  • Loss event scenarios

📊 Market Risk

⏳ Planned - Phase 4

Risk from market price movements - Multiple advanced models

  • Value at Risk (VaR)
  • Stressed VaR (Stressed)
  • Incremental Risk Charge (IRC)
  • Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega)
  • Backtesting capability

💧 Liquidity Risk

⏳ Planned - Phase 5

Risk of inability to meet funding needs - Regulatory ratios and analysis

  • Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)
  • Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)
  • Cash flow analysis
  • Maturity ladder
  • Stress scenarios

🔍 Credit Risk Approaches Comparison

📌 Note: These two approaches can be used simultaneously. The calculator allows you to add loans using either method and view portfolio summaries for each approach separately or combined.

Feature AIRB Approach Standardized Approach
Risk Parameters Internal PD, LGD, EAD models External credit ratings only
Complexity High (formulas + models) Low (lookup tables)
Risk Sensitivity Very High (continuous) Medium (discrete ratings)
Data Required 5-7 years historical data External ratings only
Regulatory Approval Required (from supervisor) Not required (Basel prescribed)
Implementation Time 6-12 months 2-4 weeks
Calculation Speed Fast Very Fast
Capital Efficiency Often Lower Often Higher
Typical RWA Range 5-300%+ 0-150%
Best For Large banks, complex portfolios Mid-size banks, simple portfolios
