📊 Schema Organization

Regulatory & Bank Management (5 tables)
  • banks — Bank master data (ID, name, code, HQ, year established)
  • branches — Bank branches (IFSC code, city, contact, parent bank)
  • regulatory_bodies — Regulators (RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, FEMA)
  • regulatory_requirements — Requirements per regulator & bank (NPA, Basel III, KYC, etc.)
  • regulatory_compliance — Compliance status tracking for each requirement
Core Banking (5 tables)
  • customers — Customer profiles (personal info, address, joined date)
  • accounts — Bank accounts (savings/current, balance, IFSC code)
  • loans — Loan records (amount, rate, classification: Standard/NPA/Doubtful/Loss)
  • transactions — Transaction history (deposits, withdrawals, EMI payments, transfers)
  • credit_risk_metrics — Customer credit risk indicators (NPA flag, DE ratio, interest coverage, etc.)
