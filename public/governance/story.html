Every status below is read live from the governance database, not asserted — if something says Gap, the system genuinely hasn't done that thing yet.

SR 11-7

US Federal Reserve / OCC guidance: banks must independently validate, document and monitor any model used to make credit decisions — not just build it well.

BASEL

The Basel Committee's model-risk principles for capital models: sound methodology, data quality, and demonstrated stress-testing before a model can size real capital.

EU AI ACT

Credit scoring is a "high-risk" AI use case under the Act: it requires human oversight, transparency to the person being scored, and continuous post-market monitoring.
