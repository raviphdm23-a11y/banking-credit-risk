Key Insights

Delta D/E Ratio:

Healthy: -0.04 (slightly reducing debt)
Default: +3.60 (dramatically increasing debt)
Difference: 3.64x worse!

Delta CIBIL:

Healthy: +0.28 (slightly improving credit)
Default: -163 (severely declining credit)
Difference: -163 points!

Interpretation:

Borrowers who default show RAPID DETERIORATION in both leverage and credit quality - these are the strongest warning signs!
