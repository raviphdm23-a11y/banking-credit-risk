2 — Financial Metrics & ML Model Features

These four variables are the only inputs to the RandomForest PD model. They must be present in every training CSV and are sent to /api/predict-pd-ml at inference time.

Categorical Senior Secured (Real Estate), Senior Secured (Financial Assets), Senior Secured (Other), Senior Unsecured, Subordinated Collateral Input

Haircut shown in parentheses		 Collateral Input

7 — Calculated Outputs (Frontend Display)
Frontend ID API Field Display Label Description & Formula Type / Unit Typical Range Five C's Usage
pdValue pd_percentage Probability of Default (PD) ML model prediction of the likelihood the borrower will default within one year. Expressed as a percentage. Source: /api/predict-pd-ml (RandomForest). Falls back to rule-based formula if API unavailable. Numeric, % 0.01% – 100%
Investment grade: < 1%		 Capacity Output
lgdValue lgd_percentage Loss Given Default (LGD) Expected percentage of EAD lost if the borrower defaults. Calculated from: base rate (by seniority) + collateral coverage adjustment + sector risk add-on. Formula: LGD = Base − (Collateral Coverage × Recovery Factor) + Sector Add-on. Numeric, % 10% – 75%
Senior secured: 10–25%; Subordinated: 60–75%		 Collateral Output
maturityValue maturity Effective Maturity (M) Remaining effective maturity of the facility in years, used in the AIRB maturity adjustment formula. Derived from loan type. Range capped at 1–5 years per Basel III. Numeric, years 0.5 – 5.0 years Conditions Output
haircutValue haircut Collateral Haircut Percentage discount applied to collateral value to account for price volatility and liquidation risk. Set by Basel III standard supervisory haircuts by collateral type. Numeric, % 0% (Cash) – 35% (Inventory) Collateral Output
Loan table: loan.riskWeight risk_weight Risk Weight (RW) AIRB: Derived from PD, LGD, correlation R, and maturity adjustment using the Basel II IRB formula: RW = f(PD, LGD, R, M) × 12.5.
SA: Looked up directly from Basel III risk weight tables by rating and exposure category.		 Numeric, % 0% (Sovereign AAA) – 150%+ (CCC/D) Capital Output
Loan table: loan.rwa
Summary: summaryTotalRWA		 rwa Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA) EAD × Risk Weight. Represents the credit-risk-adjusted asset value used as the denominator in capital ratio calculations. The key output driving regulatory capital requirements. Numeric, INR (₹) 0 – EAD × 1.5
For most loans: 20%–120% of EAD		 Capital Output
Loan table: loan.capitalRequired
Summary: summaryTotalCapital		 capital_required Capital Required Minimum regulatory capital the bank must hold against the loan: RWA × 8% (Basel III minimum Total Capital ratio). The summary panel also shows CET1 (RWA × 4.5%) and Tier 1 (RWA × 6%). Numeric, INR (₹) RWA × 8% Capital Output
8 — Portfolio Summary Outputs
Frontend ID Display Label Description & Formula Unit Five C's Usage
summaryLoanCount Loans in Portfolio Count of loans recorded in the current session. Persisted in browser localStorage. Integer Conditions Output
summaryTotalEAD Total Exposure (EAD) Sum of all loan exposure amounts in the portfolio (₹). Represents the bank's total credit exposure before risk-weighting. INR (₹) Conditions Output

9 — Five C's Summary: Variable Mapping
Five C Definition in Credit Risk Variables Mapped Role in this Model
Character Borrower's willingness to repay; track record, creditworthiness, and governance quality. borrowerId, borrowerName, bank_id, loan_id, externalRating, exposureCategory, default_flag Identification and rating-based classification. External rating directly drives SA risk weight; default flag is the evaluation label for model quality assessment.
Capacity Borrower's ability to generate cash flow sufficient to service debt obligations. interestCoverage / interest_coverage, profitabilityMargin / profitability, liquidityRatio / liquidity_ratio, pd_observed (target), pdValue (output) These three ratios are the primary drivers of PD in the RandomForest model. Low coverage, negative margins, and poor liquidity each independently raise predicted PD. Together they explain the majority of model variance.
Capital Borrower's financial strength, net worth, and equity buffer absorbing losses before default. debtToEquity / de_ratio, risk_weight, rwa / summaryTotalRWA, capital_required / summaryTotalCapital, summaryRiskDensity, summaryCET1 D/E ratio feeds directly into the PD model as a leverage signal. RWA and capital required are the bank-side capital outputs — they measure how much of the bank's own capital must be held against the loan.
Collateral Assets pledged by the borrower to secure the loan; reduces lender loss in default. seniority, collateralType, collateralValue, haircutValue, lgdValue / lgd_percentage Collateral inputs drive LGD calculation. The seniority determines the base LGD rate; collateral type sets the haircut; collateral value reduces the net loss exposure. Higher quality collateral lowers LGD and therefore lowers RWA and capital.
Conditions External environment: loan purpose, tenor, industry context, and macro conditions. sector, loanType, calcMode, exposureAmount, maturityValue, observation_date, summaryLoanCount, summaryTotalEAD Sector affects the LGD sector add-on. Loan type determines effective maturity M, which feeds into the AIRB maturity adjustment. Observation date provides temporal context for training data. Exposure amount sets the absolute scale of RWA and capital requirements.
Note on model scope: The RandomForest PD model uses only the four Capacity and Capital inputs (de_ratio, interest_coverage, profitability, liquidity_ratio). Character, Collateral, and Conditions variables are used in the rule-based LGD calculation and SA risk weight lookup, but are not currently features of the ML model. Future model iterations could incorporate sector, seniority, and maturity as additional features.
