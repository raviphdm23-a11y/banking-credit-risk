2 — ML Model Features (XGBoost — 36 features)

The active models are XGBoostClassifiers (one per exposure class — CORPORATE, SME, RETAIL_MORTGAGES, RETAIL_OTHER) trained on 36 features and targeting default_flag (binary 0/1). The four core financial ratios below are the primary explainable drivers; the remaining 32 borrower, bureau, macro, trend, and behavioural-signal features provide additional signal and are filled from application data or neutral defaults at inference time.

previous_default_flag Previous Default Flag Binary: 1 = borrower has a prior default on record, 0 = clean history. Strong Character signal; prior defaults substantially raise PD. Integer (0 or 1) Default: 0 Character Model

num_existing_products Existing Bank Products Count of active banking products (accounts, cards, deposits, investments) the borrower holds with the bank. Higher cross-sell depth typically correlates with lower churn and default risk. Integer Default: 2 Character Model

2d — Trend Features
Training CSV Column Display Label Description Type / Format Notes / Default Five C's Usage
delta_de_ratio Change in D/E Ratio Period-over-period change in the debt-to-equity ratio since origination. Positive values signal increasing leverage; negative values signal deleveraging. Captures trajectory, not just the current level. Numeric, decimal Default: 0.0 (no change) Capital Model
delta_cibil Change in CIBIL Score Period-over-period change in the bureau credit score since origination. Declining scores (negative delta) are an early warning signal even when the absolute score is still acceptable. Numeric, integer Default: 0 (no change) Character Model
months_since_origination Months Since Origination Age of the loan in months at the observation date. Captures seasoning effects — newly originated loans often show lower early-period default rates before stress materialises. Numeric, integer Default: 24 Conditions Model
delta_gdp_pct Change in GDP Growth (pp) Percentage-point change in GDP growth versus the prior year. Part of the macro regime-shift detection group — flags a country moving from expansion into slowdown. Numeric, pp Default: 0.0 (no regime shift) Conditions Model
delta_cpi_pct Change in Inflation (pp) Percentage-point change in CPI inflation versus the prior year. A sharp rise signals a fresh affordability shock beyond the absolute inflation level. Numeric, pp Default: 0.0 (no regime shift) Conditions Model
delta_policy_rate_pct Change in Policy Rate (pp) Percentage-point change in the central bank policy rate versus the prior year. Captures tightening/easing cycles that shift borrowing costs faster than the absolute rate alone shows. Numeric, pp Default: 0.0 (no regime shift) Conditions Model
delta_unemployment_pct Change in Unemployment (pp) Percentage-point change in unemployment versus the prior year. Rising unemployment trend is a leading indicator of consumer and SME stress independent of the absolute level. Numeric, pp Default: 0.0 (no regime shift) Conditions Model
macro_regime_score Macro Regime Distress Score Composite 0–100 score summarising the four macro-delta signals above into a single regime-shift indicator. 0 = normal cycle, 56+ = severe distress regime. Numeric, 0–100 Default: 0.0 (normal regime) Conditions Model

7 — Calculated Outputs (Frontend Display)
Frontend ID API Field Display Label Description & Formula Type / Unit Typical Range Five C's Usage
pdValue pd_percentage Probability of Default (PD) XGBoost model probability that the borrower will default within one year (expressed as %). Source: /api/predict-pd-ml. Falls back to rule-based formula if the model is unavailable. Numeric, % 0.01% – 100%
Investment grade: < 1%		 Capacity Output
lgdValue lgd_percentage Loss Given Default (LGD) Expected percentage of EAD lost if the borrower defaults. Calculated from: base rate (by seniority) + collateral coverage adjustment + sector risk add-on. Formula: LGD = Base − (Collateral Coverage × Recovery Factor) + Sector Add-on. Numeric, % 10% – 75%
Senior secured: 10–25%; Subordinated: 60–75%		 Collateral Output

9 — Five C's Summary: Variable Mapping
Five C Definition in Credit Risk Variables Mapped Role in this Model
Character Borrower's willingness to repay; track record, creditworthiness, and governance quality. borrowerId, borrowerName, bank_id, loan_id, externalRating, exposureCategory, default_flag (target), cibil_score, previous_default_flag, num_late_payments_past_12m, delta_cibil, months_as_customer, num_existing_products, education_enc CIBIL score and prior default history are the strongest Character signals in the XGBoost model. Bureau trend features (delta_cibil, late payments) provide early-warning signals before the absolute score deteriorates. External rating drives SA risk weight; default flag is the binary training target.
Capacity Borrower's ability to generate cash flow sufficient to service debt obligations. interestCoverage / interest_coverage, profitabilityMargin / profitability, liquidityRatio / liquidity_ratio, annual_income, foir, employment_type_enc, years_employed, num_dependents, pd_observed (legacy), pdValue (output) The three core financial ratios are the primary explainable Capacity drivers. Income, FOIR, and employment stability provide additional capacity context, especially for retail borrowers where P&L financials are unavailable. Together these features explain the majority of model variance.
Capital Borrower's financial strength, net worth, and equity buffer absorbing losses before default. debtToEquity / de_ratio, delta_de_ratio, existing_loans_count, risk_weight, rwa / summaryTotalRWA, capital_required / summaryTotalCapital, summaryRiskDensity, summaryCET1 D/E ratio and its trend (delta_de_ratio) feed directly into the model as leverage signals. RWA and capital required are the bank-side outputs — they measure how much of the bank's own capital must be held against the loan.
Collateral Assets pledged by the borrower to secure the loan; reduces lender loss in default. seniority, collateralType, collateralValue, haircutValue, lgdValue / lgd_percentage, residence_type_enc Collateral inputs drive LGD calculation (rule-based, not in XGBoost features). The seniority determines the base LGD rate; collateral type sets the haircut; collateral value reduces net loss exposure. Residence type serves as a proxy for unencumbered asset ownership.
Conditions External environment: loan purpose, tenor, industry context, and macro conditions. sector, loanType, calcMode, exposureAmount, maturityValue, loan_purpose_enc, city_tier_enc, months_since_origination, gdp_growth_pct, inflation_cpi_pct, policy_rate_pct, unemployment_pct, observation_date, summaryLoanCount, summaryTotalEAD Country macro features (GDP growth, CPI, policy rate, unemployment) bring systemic risk into the model, allowing PD to respond to the borrower's economic environment. Loan purpose and city tier contextualise the borrower's local conditions. Sector affects LGD via the sector risk add-on (not a direct XGBoost feature).
Model scope note: The active models are per-exposure-class XGBoostClassifiers (CORPORATE, SME, RETAIL_MORTGAGES, RETAIL_OTHER) trained on 36 features targeting default_flag (binary, RBI 90-day NPA rule). The four core ratios (de_ratio, interest_coverage, profitability, liquidity_ratio) remain the primary explainable drivers and are the only features collected on the borrower assessment form. The remaining 32 features are sourced from application records or filled with calibrated neutral defaults via feature_meta.model_feature_frame(). Collateral and SA inputs are used exclusively in rule-based LGD and SA risk weight calculations, which run independently of the ML model.
