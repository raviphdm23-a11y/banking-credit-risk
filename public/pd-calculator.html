📐 Calculation Methodology

Rule-Based PD Model

The probability of default is calculated using a weighted scoring model based on four key financial metrics:

PD (%) = Base Rate + Leverage Impact + Profitability Impact + Liquidity Impact + Coverage Impact Where: • Base Rate = 2.0% (long-term average default rate) • Leverage Impact = (D/E Ratio) × 0.8 • Profitability Impact = Max(0, -Margin% × 0.15) • Liquidity Impact = Max(0, (1.5 - Current Ratio) × 3.0) • Coverage Impact = Max(0, (4.0 - Interest Coverage) × 0.5)

Variable Definitions

  • Base Rate (2.0%): Historical average default probability
  • Debt-to-Equity (D/E): Leverage risk - higher leverage increases default risk
  • Profitability Margin: Profitability - lower profitability increases default risk
  • Liquidity Ratio: Ability to meet short-term obligations
  • Interest Coverage: Ability to service debt from EBITDA

Key Assumptions

  • Model is calibrated for medium to large corporate exposures
  • Financial metrics are annual figures
  • Model assumes normal economic conditions
  • Industry-specific adjustments may be needed
  • Qualitative factors should complement this model
